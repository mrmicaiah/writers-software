## Writing Rules Summary

- **POV:** First person for Garrison's death ONLY. Third person limited for rest of book.
- **Primary POV:** Ryan (~70%), with cutaways to Professor and Pickler. Que is NEVER a POV character.
- **Style:** Clean, direct, Grisham. Psychology through action, not narration.
- **Profanity:** Hell, damn, bastard allowed sparingly. No F-word, C-word, or blasphemy.

See `style-guide.md` for full details.
