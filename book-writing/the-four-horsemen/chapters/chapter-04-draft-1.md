# CHAPTER 4

"What the hell is this Frank?" He barked, pushing open the door to the captain's office.

"Ummm, excuse me, first of all, how many times have I told you to knock before you come in? And second, why do I have to keep reminding you to refer to me as Captain and not Frank?" He yelled, finishing his thought with a frustrated slap on his desk.

"Right, sorry bout that." Tom replied, lowering his tone.

"Now, what are you referring to?"

"This case my secretary just gave me."

There was a long pause as the captain took a deep breath and let it out.

"Tom, you know she's not…" he paused with another frustrated sigh, and then continued. "Never mind. That's the case that just came in, the insurance company for the park knows the commissioner personally and he's putting a lot of pressure on us to make something of the case."

"It's a dead guy who croaked on a coaster for Pete's sake."

"The witnesses said he was lively before the ride and then fell asleep as the ride climbed to the tallest peak."

"Lots of men are sissies Fran… I mean Captain, doesn't mean it was some wild murder."

"Detective, it doesn't mean he was a sissy either, and there's no way of knowing whether he died from sheer fear or from choking on a blasted bug just by looking at that one page report. I want you to give this case real attention before we sign off on it as just a death by natural causes. The man was a prominent executive for Capital Investments, and they are demanding answers as well."

"Captain this seems."

"Detective," the captain interrupted, "go down to Kings Dominion, ask some questions and do a little legwork on the victim. I need to know we did our best on this one and since Detective Swooner is full up, you are (much to my dismay) my only hope."

The room seemed to be closing in on Tom as he realized he was losing the battle he had started. He closed the file and retreated from the office.

"Close my door please!"

But Tom kept walking all the way back to his office where he tossed the file like an old rag into the air like all his other ones, as he reached for his coat.

---

Pickler tossed his coat on the passenger's seat of his truck and climbed in.

A few miles down the road he had the sheriff on the phone. The conversation was short, as he tried to figure out if there was anything out of the ordinary at the park. The sheriff shortly remarked how he had done all the preliminary work and he'd give it to him when he arrived.

He cranked up the radio as he tried to forget about the giant waste of time the case he was headed out to.

He parked by the front gate where the sheriff's car was lit up. The sheriff met him at the gate and motioned for him as he walked over.

"Let's make this quick sheriff, there's a burrito with my name on it waiting at the taqueria."

"Yea, yea, I'll see what I can do."

They pushed through the gate and made their way through the park to the coaster.

"The coroner is wanting to move the body and obviously the park is wanting their coaster back."

"No bodies getting in the way of business huh?" Pickler laughed.

"Honestly, I'm not sure why they even sent you over here, seems open and shut."

Pickler rolled his eyes, he had been thinking the same thing for the past hour.

"Someone up top has more power than we got time apparently."

The sheriff handed him the report as they walked.

"Any witnesses?" Pickler asked, scanning through the papers.

"He died on a roller coaster, everyone was screaming in terror, the only witnesses are the ones who noticed the one guy sleeping."

"Sleeping?"

"Nobody noticed anything, the only real witness we had was some people laughing about the guy who fell asleep on the ride. That was our guy, Clyde Garrison."

"Why do people come to these places anyway? Bunch of cattle all led to long lines where big business takes their money and gives them nothing."

The sheriff shrugged, and motioned for one of his deputies as they approached the yellow caution tape.

"Show Detective Pickler around, and give him whatever he needs." He gave Pickler a see-you-around look, and walked away.

"What can I do for you detective?"

"I want to talk to this Kevin kid, is he still here?"

He paused. "Yea, you could say that. He's a little out of it."

"What's that mean?" Pickler asked, following the deputy.

"Shock or something, the paramedics said he probably wouldn't say much for a while."

"A wuss huh?"

"Looks like a kid in college, don't reckon he's seen many dead bodies."

Kevin was sitting down at a picnic table near the ride entrance. A deputy stood behind him a few paces as if he was guarding him.

Pickler stopped in front of the table and waved his hand to the deputy opposite the table.

"Give us some space will ya?"

The deputy nodded and walked some distance away.

Pickler slapped the file down in front of the kid and he jumped.

"What the heck dude?"

"Oh, did I startle you?"

Kevin shrugged and went back to looking at the table.

"We can make this quick and I can get you cleared to go home. Would you like that?"

Kevin shrugged, no response.

Pickler slid into the seat across from the kid, he felt a little sorry for him. Seemed like a good kid who had seen something terrible.

"I just need to ask you a few questions, nothing serious. You OK with that?"

Kevin looked up and shrugged again.

"Did you talk to the victim before the ride?"

Kevin continued to look down, his eyes glued to the table as if he was still looking at the dead body.

"Were you the one who checked his seat, did he say anything to you?"

Kevin shrugged again, and Pickler was losing his patience.

"Sounds to me like you're the one who killed him." He said, closing the file abruptly.

Kevin jumped, and looked up.

"Now wait a second mister. All I did was buckle him in, he didn't say nothing to me, and I as sure as I'm sitting here didn't kill him."

"I don't believe you." Pickler barked back.

"He was so happy, there was nothing wrong with him, it's so terrible. You gotta do something."

Kevin reached out and grabbed Pickler by the arm. He jumped and pushed the kid back, the deputy dashed across the grass from his post and snatched the kid's arm.

"What the freak kid?" Pickler said as he jumped out of the picnic table.

"I swear I didn't do it." Kevin yelled and jerked his arms back and forth as the big deputy pulled him out of the picnic table and wrecked him to the ground.

"You need to calm down son." He said, as he wrestled his hands behind his back.

"I didn't do it!" He continued yelling over and over again.

Pickler was used to getting under people's skin, but this was something else. The kid needed serious therapy. Either way, he wasn't getting anything else out of him.

He walked back over to the coaster and looked around.
