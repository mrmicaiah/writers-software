## Overview of Plot

A man with a strict code operates under the radar killing for wealthy business men with a few select hit men, spends years saving and doing horrible things for his chance to leave the country and enjoy the good life.

As he gets closer to having the money he needs to do it, his wife resents him, his boss hates him, and he grows more and more impatient.

When offered a chance to make 10xs what he usually makes to kill someone outside of his strict code, he refuses. But then learns he's been robbed. Half of the money he was using for his escape is now gone. With his impatience growing, he accepts the offer to kill the other guy on short notice. When his contractors all refuse and cut ties with him for being stupid, he's now faced with losing everything or going through with the contract.

Armed with a few recommendations for street hitman, he goes through two hitman before he finds one he trusts. But in his search he tells the guy too much info about himself.

## Story Summary — Four Horsemen Concept

Haunted by the label "killer," a man forms an elite assassination syndicate: The Four Horsemen. Each member represents a facet of death—public violence, accidental demise, subtle poison, and psychological ruin. As their enterprise grows, the founder's vision of control is tested by the dark desires and traumas each Horseman brings to the group. The story becomes a study of guilt, compulsion, and the seductive power of identity, as the Horsemen are forced to confront not only their enemies, but the monsters within themselves.

---

## Character Breakdown (Original Concept)

**The Founder / The Pale Horseman (Natural Causes)**
- Role: Mastermind, specializes in deaths that appear natural.
- Obsessive need for control and perfection.
- Rationalizes killing as a service, but secretly craves the power of life and death.
- Suffers insomnia, guilt dreams, and moments of dissociation.

**The Red Horseman (Public Violence)**
- Specialty: Messy, public killings.
- Thrives on chaos and attention; sees violence as self-expression.
- Secretly fears anonymity and irrelevance.
- Prone to manic highs and depressive crashes after each job.

**The Black Horseman (Accident Maker)**
- Specialty: Murders staged as accidents.
- Perfectionist, obsessed with the artistry of a perfect crime.
- Struggles with guilt over collateral damage.
- Compulsively replays mistakes in their mind, suffers from intrusive thoughts.

**The White Horseman (Mindbreaker)**
- Specialty: Psychological attacks—incapacitates, drives victims insane.
- Deeply empathetic, ironically; fascinated by the mind's fragility.
- Struggles with guilt and self-loathing, often projects onto victims.
- Keeps journals of each "ruined" mind, seeking meaning in the suffering caused.

## Opening — Disturbance Draft

I didn't know it at the time but it was my turn to die. I wasn't a serial killer, I wasn't bad, in fact I taught kids how to be better in society. Nothing about my life consequented a death sentence in my mind at least. But in the eyes of God I had done something worthy of death. It was just one thing, well maybe more. My mom thought being a teacher would be less dangerous, I thought the same thing. I climbed onto the coaster at Kings Dominion. The two girls in front of me were attractive, and smiled as they climbed in. I was just trying to have a relaxing day away from work. My boss actually recommended that I come here, I don't know if he knew this is where they were going to kill me, but it does seem a bit ironic. The coaster began to move and I could feel a heaviness set into my chest. I'm not an overweight guy, but I suppose I'm as heavy as any man with a job in front of a computer who does very little exercise. The heaviness seemed a bit like dread from the coaster ride to come. But as we neared the top of the first drop my breathing was almost completely stopped. The coaster released from the top of the bend and the shock plus whatever I had been poisoned with stopped my breathing altogether. I couldn't scream, there was no one to help me, and as far as anyone was concerned, I was just another scared person on a coaster. I certainly wasn't a man who couldn't breathe, a man suffocating while everyone else was having fun. Then, all was dark.
